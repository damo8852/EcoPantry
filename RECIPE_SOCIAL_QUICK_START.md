# Recipe Social Platform - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 How to Use the New Recipe Features

### Accessing Recipes
1. **Open the hamburger menu** (≡) from the home screen
2. **Tap "Recipes"** to open the Recipes Hub

### Recipes Hub - Three Main Options:

---

## 📝 **1. Create Recipe**
Share your own recipes with the community!

**Steps:**
1. Tap "Create Recipe" card
2. Fill in recipe details:
   - Recipe Name (required)
   - Description (optional but recommended)
   - Prep Time (e.g., "15min")
   - Cook Time (e.g., "30min")
   - Servings (e.g., "4")

3. Add Ingredients:
   - Tap "+ Add Ingredient" for more fields
   - Include quantities (e.g., "2 cups rice")

4. Add Instructions:
   - Write step-by-step directions
   - Tap "+ Add Step" for more steps

5. Select Options:
   - **Cooking Tool**: Pan, Air Fryer, Rice Cooker, etc.
   - **Recipe Style**: Creative, Healthy, Quick, etc.
   - **Cuisine Type**: Asian, Italian, Mexican, etc.

6. **Anonymous Mode** (optional):
   - Toggle ON to post as "Anonymous"
   - Your identity will be hidden

7. Tap "Create Recipe" to publish!
   - Saved to community feed
   - Also saved to your personal collection

---

## 📚 **2. My Recipes**
View and organize all your recipes in one place!

**Features:**
- See both **user-created** and **AI-generated** recipes
- Tap **filter icon** (⚙️) to sort by:
  - Source (My Creations / AI Generated)
  - Cooking Tool
  - Recipe Style
  - Cuisine Type
- Swipe to delete unwanted recipes
- Tap any recipe to view full details

**Filter Tips:**
- Combine multiple filters for precise results
- Orange dot on filter icon = filters active
- Tap "Clear" to reset all filters

---

## 🌍 **3. Community Recipes**
Discover amazing recipes from other users!

**Browsing:**
- Tap **sort icon** (⋮) to change sorting:
  - **Recent**: Newest recipes first
  - **Most Saved**: Popular recipes
  - **Top Rated**: Highest rated recipes

**Recipe Cards Show:**
- Author name (or "Anonymous")
- Star rating and number of ratings
- Save count
- Cooking time
- Cooking tool and style tags
- Quick preview info

**Interactions:**

### ⭐ **Rating Recipes**
1. Tap "Rate" button on any recipe
2. Tap stars to select 1-5 rating
3. Tap "Submit"
4. Your rating updates the average instantly

**Note:** Guest users cannot rate recipes

### 💬 **Comments**
1. Tap "Comments" button
2. Browse existing comments
3. Type your comment at bottom
4. Tap send icon (➤)

**Note:** Guest users cannot comment

### 📌 **Saving Recipes**
1. Tap bookmark icon on recipe card
2. Recipe saved to your personal collection
3. Access anytime from "My Recipes"

### 📖 **View Full Recipe**
1. Tap "View" button
2. See complete ingredients list
3. Read step-by-step instructions
4. Swipe down to close

---

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### For Best Results:
- **Be Detailed**: Include quantities and specific instructions
- **Use Tags**: Select cooking tool and style for better discovery
- **Engage**: Rate and comment on recipes you try
- **Stay Anonymous**: Use anonymous mode for privacy when sharing

### Finding Recipes:
- Use filters in "My Recipes" to organize your collection
- Sort community feed by "Top Rated" for tried-and-true recipes
- Sort by "Recent" to discover new creations
- Check the stats (ratings, saves) to gauge popularity

### Anonymous Posting:
- Perfect for sharing family recipes without attribution
- Your account remains private
- Still counts toward your personal recipe collection
- Can be used for sensitive or personal recipes

---

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

**"Guest users cannot rate/comment"**
- You're signed in as a guest
- Sign up for a full account to interact

**"Recipe already saved"**
- Recipe is already in your collection
- Check "My Recipes" to view it

**No recipes showing in community**
- Be the first to share!
- Create a recipe to start the community

**Filters not working**
- Recipes need metadata (tool, style, cuisine) to be filtered
- AI-generated recipes may not have all metadata
- User-created recipes have metadata if you filled it in

---

## 📊 **Understanding the System**

### Recipe Sources:
- **AI Generated**: Created by the app's AI from your ingredients
- **User Created**: Made by you or other community members

### Recipe Metadata:
When you create a recipe or AI generates one, it can include:
- Cooking tool preference (affects recipe type)
- Recipe style (cooking approach)
- Cuisine type (flavor profile)

These help with filtering and discovery!

### Rating System:
- 1 star = Poor
- 2 stars = Fair
- 3 stars = Good
- 4 stars = Very Good
- 5 stars = Excellent

Be honest and constructive in your ratings!
